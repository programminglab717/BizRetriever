Wholesalers and distributors sell to other businesses (shops, cafés, salons, other traders) on account,
and usually keep these files:
- An invoice or sales-order export from their accounts software (QuickBooks, Xero, Sage) or a
  hand-kept invoice sheet: one row per item invoiced, with the invoice number, date, customer and due
  date repeated on every row or written only on the first row of each invoice. A "Paid?" or "Status"
  column says whether it has been paid. "Backordered" or "To follow" means items still to be sent.
- A customer (account) list: one row per trade customer, with an account number, contact details,
  payment terms (like "30 days" or "Net 30"), a credit limit, the sales rep and the price list.
- A payments received sheet: date, customer, invoice number and amount.
- A stock list with product codes, case sizes, costs, trade prices and how many are in the warehouse.
- Supplier (purchase) orders: one row per product ordered, with the supplier, dates, quantity and cost.
- An expenses sheet, often with months as columns.
Totals, subtotals, notes and blank rows are common and are not records. The same customer is often
spelled several ways ("Bloom & Co", "Bloom and Co Ltd"). Dates may be day-first or month-first.
